Three steps, once a day

What used to take an hour with a calculator and WhatsApp messages.

  1. Insert approval documents

    Pick one Excel job list or several at once: Date, Job No, Driver, Insurance Approved, SST. Extra columns such as Claim No or Workshop come along into the report.

  2. Type each driver's %

    Every driver in the files gets a box. Type this batch's percentage. The last one you used shows as a hint, never filled in for you.

  3. Calculate and approve

    See the pay per driver, the held jobs and a slip for each driver. Approve to record the jobs as paid, then download the pay sheet for Excel.

Built so nobody is paid wrong

The app never guesses. Anything unclear is held with the reason, and you fix it in the sheet.

Held

SST left blank

A blank is never treated as 0. Type 0 when a job has no SST.

Held

Already paid

Every approved job number is remembered online, so the same job can't be paid twice from any device.

Held

Same job number twice

Both copies are held, with the file and row of each, so you can see which is right.

Held

No % or a wrong %

A blank box, or 0.18 typed instead of 18, holds that driver's jobs until it's fixed.

Check

Likely typos

SST above 8% of the approved amount, or a job older than 7 days, is paid but flagged.

Check

New names

A driver name the app hasn't seen is marked New, so a spelling slip doesn't start a second driver.
