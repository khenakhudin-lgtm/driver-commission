Put the icon on your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it may be under the … menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Check the name and the orange % icon, then tap Add.

Tapping the icon opens this page. Tap Open Driver Commission to go to the app. You need to be signed in to Claude with access to the app.
